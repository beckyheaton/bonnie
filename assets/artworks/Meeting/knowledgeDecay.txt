I’ve stumbled into a meeting about knowledge decay. “Everything decays” my colleague tells me. I realise that I don’t want to be in this meeting anymore so I stand up to leave. “Everything decays” he says again, and though I know he isn’t actually speaking to me, and that this meeting is about training graduate engineers and has nothing to do with my life, when he looks at me and says “There is no one problem, just a constant battle”, I believe him.
