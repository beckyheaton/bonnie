


THINGS YOU MIGHT BE MISSING
============================

- Image hosting decision: are you hosting images locally in the repo (e.g.
  pushed to GitHub) or using an external host (e.g. Cloudinary)? This affects
  what the final "path" in your JSON looks like. Decide before step 5.

- Image sizing/optimisation: placeholder images won't reveal if large
  images slow down the site. Worth using realistically-sized images now,
  not tiny test thumbnails.
